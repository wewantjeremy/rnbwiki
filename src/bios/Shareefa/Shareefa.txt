	Shareefa (born Shareefa Faradah Cooper on March 12, 1984) is an American R&B singer best known for her 2006 hit single "Need a Boss" (featuring Ludacris). Signed to Ludacris's Def Jam-affiliated label, Disturbing tha Peace, she released her debut album Point of No Return in 2006. Born in East Orange, New Jersey, and raised in New Jersey and Charlotte, North Carolina, Shareefa was discovered by iconic R&B producer Teddy Riley. Her gritty vocal style and early features on the Disturbing tha Peace albums gained her significant traction in the mid-2000s.
