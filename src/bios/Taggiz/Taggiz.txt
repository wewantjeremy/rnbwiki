	Taggiz is an independent R&B solo artist and singer-songwriter from Omaha, Nebraska (often known fully as Taggiz Shackelford). He is well-known in the Midwest music scene for his melodic vocals and distinct fusion of hip-hop and R&B styles. Taggiz has built a loyal following with popular tracks like "She's Gone" and "Lets Ride". His extensive R&B discography includes catalog bodies of work like V.S.O.P. 2 Emotions of a Rollercoaster.
