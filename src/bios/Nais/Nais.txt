	Nais was an R&B group from the ’90s. Not much information is given about them, but they were signed to the Street Life Records label. In 1995, they released their debut and only album, “Str-8 From Da’ Boot.” It’s unknown how or if the album charted at all.
	The tracks “69B (Alizè & Me),” a cover version of Frankie Valli’s song, “My Eyes Adored You” and “Break U Off” were released as singles, but didn’t make the charts at all.
