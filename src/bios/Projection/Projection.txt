	Projection is a UK street-soul and R&B band from the 1980s. The group is best known for their timeless 1986 tracks "Lovestruck" and "Turn Your Love (Right Around)", which remain highly sought-after staples in the rare groove and neo-soul communities. 
	Projection's Signature Tracks: "Lovestruck", originally released on Elite Records, is the group's most celebrated record. It blends elegant, synth-heavy production with a classic street soul vocal. "Turn Your Love (Right Around)" (1986): A bouncy, mid-tempo R&B and boogie jam known for its smooth, nostalgic groove. "What's Your Problem" (1988): Produced by the late Andy Sojka, this later release pushed the band's signature sound further into late-80s soul and funk.
