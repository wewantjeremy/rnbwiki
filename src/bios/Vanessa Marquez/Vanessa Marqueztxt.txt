Vanessa Marquez is an American R&B singer formerly signed to Star Trak Entertainment, the record label owned by Pharrell Williams & Chad Hugo Also Known As The Neptunes. It seems she recorded quite a few tracks during her tenure, including "Want You To Know" featuring Justin Timberlake, "If You Keep On Askin'", and "Friends Are" featuring Pharrell. The only track that saw the light of day was "Good Girl" from the 2003 album The Neptunes Presents...Clones. She may be best known as the female singer on Justin Timberlake's hit 2002 song "Rock Your Body."
