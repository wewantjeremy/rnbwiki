	Anjel was a quartet formed when Destiny's Child stars LaTavia Roberson and LeToya Luckett were kicked from the group and decided to start their own one, thus was formed Anjel. Originally, it comprised of LaTavia, LeToya, Natasha Ramos and Tiffany Beaudoin. Natasha was later kicked out because of her lies and her inability to get on with Tiffany. Her replacement Naty Quinones, a singer with sexy vocals and a skilful rapping ability, entered the group. Before the group could debut, their label fell through and each lady pursued her own solo interest.
