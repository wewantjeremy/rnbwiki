	The Irvin Lee Project, led by Irvin Lee, released an eponymous album in 1987 and disappeared from the scene. Sought after by crate diggers, the project had a rotation of singers including Antonio Cole, Chip Landry, Tracy Hamlin, Trisie Gee and Darryl Hamlin. Irvin Lee previously was a member of Terry Huff & Special Delivery, the Washington, D.C. soul group responsible for The Lonely One in the 1970s.
