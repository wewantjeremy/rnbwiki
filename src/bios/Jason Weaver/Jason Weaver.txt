	Jason Weaver (born July 18, 1979) is an American actor and singer, best known for playing the young Michael Jackson in The Jacksons: An American Dream. He also starred in the sitcoms Thea and Smart Guy, and notably provided the singing voice for Young Simba in Disney’s 1994 animated film The Lion King.
	Born in Chicago, Illinois, Weaver was immersed in the entertainment industry early. His mother, Kitty Haywood, was a notable studio singer and jingle writer who helped guide his career. Weaver made his acting debut on Oprah Winfrey's 1990 television series Brewster Place. Shortly after, he landed the career-defining role of a young Michael Jackson in the 1992 miniseries The Jacksons: An American Dream, which garnered him widespread critical acclaim.
	Weaver successfully transitioned into teen and adult acting roles. He starred as Marcus Henderson on the hit WB sitcom Smart Guy (1997–1999). He has also appeared in several prominent films, including Drumline (2002), The Ladykillers (2004), and ATL (2006). In addition, he holds a recurring role as Rashaad "Shaad" Marshall on the Showtime drama series The Chi.As a singer, Weaver provided the iconic vocals for "I Just Can't Wait to Be King" in The Lion King. Rather than accepting a $2 million flat fee for his work on the movie, his mother negotiated a royalty deal that has provided him ongoing residuals over the decades. He has also released R&B music under the stage name J-Weav, most notably featuring on the hit single "One Call Away" with Chingy in 2003.
