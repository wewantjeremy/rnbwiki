	TiffanyJ is an indie soul and R&B artist known for her soulful and melodic music. She has a multifaceted career that includes being a singer, songwriter, author, and entrepreneur. TiffanyJ has a background rooted in the church, particularly at the First Nazareth Baptist Church, where she was exposed to music from a young age. She has also been a part of the award-winning urban inspirational group "Eternal Voices."
	In addition to her music career, TiffanyJ is recognized for her work in promoting self-esteem and empowerment among young individuals, particularly young women. She launched the "Beauty, You Are Boot Camp," a self-esteem movement aimed at inspiring and uplifting young queens. TiffanyJ has released music albums, including "Solbird Sessions Live," which achieved success by reaching #8 on the iTunes R&B/Soul charts. She is also an author, having written the book "It All Starts with Me: An Interactive Guide to Discovering Self and Loving It."
	Her creative endeavors extend beyond music and writing, as she has created fictional characters, Super Beauty and Handsome Hero, and developed plush dolls that serve as motivational pep talkers. TiffanyJ has made appearances on television, including BET's "Grand Hustle" and "Good Morning America," and has been featured in publications like Black Enterprise, Essence, and Hype Hair.
	TiffanyJ is actively involved in charitable and community work, serving on various committees and nonprofit boards. She has received recognition for her contributions to the music and arts community, including being awarded "Best Artist" at the Columbia Neighborhood Awards. Her commitment to personal growth and creativity continues to shape her ever-evolving artistic journey.
