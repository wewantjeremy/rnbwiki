	3pc. is an R&B trio from Chicago. In 2002 they opened for Brain Mckinght and B2K. The group consists of Maurice aka Reese G,Jason aka JB, and Martin aka Lucky. They were signed to 312 Entertainment. Through this label they released their self-titled debut in 2001, which sold 35,000 copies. This release spawned the single "Ooh Ah," which peaked as the no. 1 requested song on WGCI and Power 92 Chicago, and got airplay on 106 & Park.
