	Mason is an R&B trio is a sibling vocal-instrumental group from Tulsa, Oklahoma. Brothers Gary, Tre (Clifford), and Tony Mason released their 1987 debut album Livin' on the Edge on Elektra/Warner Bros., landing a #38 Billboard hit with "Double Exposure" and a #2 Billboard Dance hit with "Pour It On".Key Tony Mason served as the lead vocalist and guitarist. He has extensive industry experience, notably touring as a background vocalist for Charlie Wilson on The Culture Tour and appearing on NBC's The Voice. Gary Mason was the bass guitarist for the trio while Clifford "Tre" Mason was the lead guitarist for the trio
