	Michon Young is an award-winning, Gary, Indiana-born and Detroit-based singer, songwriter, and producer. Known for her signature smooth and sultry voice, she blends gospel, R&B, and jazz influences. She has been singing since age 12 and was discovered by gospel icon Pastor Marvin L. Winans. Her musical career spans acclaimed international tours and massive performances at venues like the Hartford Jazz Fest and the Taste of Chicago. She was hand-selected to sing background for Marvin L. Winans' debut solo concert, and performed a duet with him at the Grand Opening of Tyler Perry Studios in Atlanta. Her 2015 track "Always Remember" hit #1 on the UK's soul and jazz charts, and her single "Honeydew" earned Billboard recognition. She is known for projects like Possessing the Promise (2011) and Love Is My Lane (2024), which features hit singles like "Bounce Back". She was named Best Female Soul Artist of the Year and Indie Artist Female of the Year by the UK's Groundbreakin Awards in 2019.
