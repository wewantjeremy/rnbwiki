	Paradise (often stylized as whoisPARADISE or paradise_mn) is a rising genre-bending R&B artist and singer-songwriter. Discovered and signed to the joint venture of Usher, L.A. Reid, and gamma., he has quickly been making a name for himself by "bringing love back to R&B" with his soulful, emotion-driven vocals and modern pop-R&B sound. His deep connection to music began around age four or five after being inspired by the showmanship and artistry of Michael Jackson. By age 15, he decided to pursue music seriously, uploading his own personal records to SoundCloud while juggling music with playing basketball. Blending traditional soul influences with contemporary beats, his signature vocal delivery is smooth, expressive, and focused heavily on themes of love and relationship dynamics. "Highway" was his standout single (released via the mega and gamma. labels). In interviews detailing his signing, he has noted the surreal and validating experience of playing his unreleased material for R&B icon Usher, who connected deeply with his artistic direction.
