	Tasha Holiday is an American contemporary R&B singer who was signed to MCA Records in the 1990s. Her biggest success was with the single "Just the Way You Like It" which peaked in the top thirty of the Billboard R&B singles chart, #93 on the Hot 100 and became one of BET's most played music videos. Billboard Magazine called her album Just the Way You Like It "a promising debut". One version features Lil' Cease and another features Mase. It was produced by Stevie J and features uncredited vocals from Kelly Price, who wrote and produced the track alongside Stevie J and Clarence Emery. Holiday also sang vocals on the single "Don't You Worry" by reggae artist Ruffa.
	In 2006, she appeared on the track "Life So Real" on the Lil' O album Neva Lay Down Vol. 1. In 2011, she worked with producer Mike Nitty and a single, "I Used To Love You" was released on YouTube on June 22, 2011. They also premiered a song "They Want Me to Stop" earlier that year. In 2014, two more collaborations between Nitty and Holiday were released on Nitty's SoundCloud account. The following year, she collaborated with Nookie on the song "Dreams."
