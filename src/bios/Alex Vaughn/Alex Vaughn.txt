	Alex Vaughn is an American R&B singer, songwriter, and pianist from Prince George's County, Maryland. Known for her classically trained vocals and vulnerable songwriting, she gained widespread prominence after signing with LVRN/Interscope Records in 2021 and releasing her breakthrough EP, The Hurtbook. Born Alexandria Von Wallace, she initially forfeited a full college music scholarship to pursue a solo career. She honed her craft working as a background singer for artists like Goapele and Cece Peniston. Following independent projects like The 4pm Mix (2017) and The Shift (2018), her career reached new heights after her signing. She has since collaborated with prominent R&B figures, including Ari Lennox, Summer Walker, and Muni Long.
