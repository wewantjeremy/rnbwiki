	RAaB (born Robert Stevenson in Florida) was a male R&B singer from the 90’s. Not much information is given about him, but he was signed to Rip-It Records.
	In 1993, he released his only album “You’re the One” which peaked at #31 on Billboard’s Heatseekers Albums chart and #67 on Billboard’s R&B Albums chart. His first single “Foreplay” peaked at #74 on the Billboard Hot 100 and #22 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 20 weeks. His follow-up single “Feel Me” peaked at #97 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for only 2 weeks.
	RAaB was featured on the 69 Boyz’s song “I Need You” on their 1998 album, “The Wait is Over.”
