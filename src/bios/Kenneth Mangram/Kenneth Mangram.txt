	Kenneth Mangram (born in Vernon, Texas) was an R&B singer-songwriter from the ’90s. Not much information is given about him, but he was signed to Qwest Records. In 1995, he released his only album “Intertwine Da’ Fold” which didn’t appear to make any album charts at all. His first single “I’ll Send You Roses” peaked at #83 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 6 weeks, making it his only highest chart appearance on the R&B chart to date.
	Kenneth’s follow-up single “What’s Yo’ Name” peaked at #92 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for only 3 weeks. After that, it seems as if Kenneth has faded into obscurity. It’s unknown what he’s up to these days.
