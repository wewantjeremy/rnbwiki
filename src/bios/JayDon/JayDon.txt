	JayDon (born Jaydon Howard McCrary on July 18, 2007) is an American singer, dancer, and actor. Best known for voicing Young Simba in Disney's The Lion King (2019) and starring in The Paynes, he recently signed with Usher and L.A. Reid’s Mega label to release his debut full-length album, Me My Songs & I.A Los Angeles native, JayDon began his career at age 8 with a guest appearance on Disney's K.C. Undercover. His musical breakthrough followed when he collaborated with Childish Gambino on the Grammy-nominated track "Terrified". By age 10, he became the youngest male solo artist signed to Hollywood Records, releasing his debut EP Shine in 2019. In 2025, he transitioned into a mature R&B sound with his debut album and hit singles like "Ah! Ah!" and "Lullaby". His musical style blends modern R&B with classic soul, deeply influenced by icons like Usher, Michael Jackson, and Chris Brown.
