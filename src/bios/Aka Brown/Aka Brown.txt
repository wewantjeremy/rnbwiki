	AKA Brown was a New Zealand music group comprised of John Chongnee an Sam Feo II. They achieved success in their native country. AKA Brown was first heard fronting the Urban Pacifika single ‘One’ in 1999, and then went on to release their first single, ‘Something I Need’ in late 1999.
	Shortly after Urban Pacifika released an album featuring three of their songs, plus another song with rapper Dei Hamo.
	John Chongnee notes his primary music influences as Guy, Jodeci, Stevie Wonder, and Jodeci’s producer DeVante. AKA Brown are great live performers have enjoyed opening gigs for top international artists Next, Usher and Hip Hop superstar Snoop Dogg.
