	Milira Jones, known professionally as Milira, is an American R&B and soul singer who gained prominence in the early 1990s. Known for her warm, jazz-influenced vocal delivery and skilled scatting, she was famously dubbed the "19-year-old Sarah Vaughan" by Motown Records executives. Milira was born on September 27, 1969, in Hollis, New York. She inherited a rich musical lineage: her father, Eddie Jones, was a member of early doo-wop groups like The Demens, and her mother, Barbara Joyner, sang in the 1960s girl group The Veneers. After singing in church and performing studio background vocals as a child, Milira's major breakthrough came in the late 1980s when she won Amateur Night at the legendary Apollo Theater. Her victory caught the attention of music executives, leading to a contract with Apollo Theatre Records, which was distributed globally by Motown Records.
