	Jaz Karis is a South London singer and songwriter known for her rich, soulful voice and genre-blending sound. An alumnus of the prestigious BRIT School, she seamlessly merges classic R&B and soul with contemporary Afrobeats, reggae, and amapiano. She has been praised for her vulnerable storytelling and vibrant live performances. Born Jasmine Winifred, her musical journey began in her local church choir and mastering classical piano. 
	After graduating from the BRIT School—the creative arts institution that also produced Adele and Amy Winehouse—she broke out commercially with her 2017 debut EP, Into the Wilderness. Her ballad "Petty Lover" went viral a year later, establishing her as a formidable force in the UK R&B scene. In the years following, she steadily built her catalog with EPs like Summer Stories (2019), All Eyes on U (2020), and Dear Jaz (2022). During the pandemic, she expanded her artistry by writing, producing, and engineering her own projects. She has collaborated with prominent artists such as Juls, Kojey Radical, and Mahalia. Her highly anticipated debut album, Safe Flight, draws from her life and travels between London and Los Angeles.
