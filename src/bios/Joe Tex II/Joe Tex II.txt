	Joe Tex II is a Texas-based soul singer and the son of the legendary 1960s and 1970s R&B icon Joe Tex (Joseph Arrington Jr.). Carrying on his family's musical legacy, he released his debut CD, The Legacy Continues, blending original tracks with his father's classic material.
	Born Joseph Arrington in Baytown, Texas, Joe Tex II grew up heavily influenced by his father's stage presence and unique blend of Southern soul, gospel, and country-infused rap. After his father passed away in 1982, Arrington worked to preserve and build upon the family's R&B heritage.
	In 2004, he made a major splash in the Southern soul scene by launching his own label, Legacy Records. He released The Legacy Continues, which featured five original songs he wrote himself and a fresh remake of the classic Joe Tex track "I Want to Do for You". He regularly collaborated with family members, including cousins Vince and Donald Victor, and formed a touring band to bring back the classic soul revue-style shows his father was famous for.
