	Sat-R-Day, originally, Clarence York (See), Mitchel Kroon (Crown), and Ivan Peroti (I.V.) is a Dutch R&B group. Debuting in 1998, this trio had hits in 7 different countries, toured across Europe, and sold over 300.000 copies of "(I'm Digging Your) Steelo". In 2000, they released The Weekend is For You. I.V. announced his departure in Oct 2002 due to personal issues.
	After numerous auditions and screenings they still hadn't found the right substitute for I.V. One day Crown told See that his younger brother E.J. was singing in an R&B group, but that the group at that time didn't have any potential. E.J. subsequently joined Sat-R-Day.
