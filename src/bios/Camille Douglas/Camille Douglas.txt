	Camille Douglas was a Canadian R&B singer from Toronto who emerged from the city's R&B scene in the 1990s. She performed regularly at Toronto's Bump'N'Hustle showcase, won the Rising Star Competition's talent-search division in 1995, and later placed second at Amateur Night at the Apollo. Douglas also opened for artists including Monica, Doug E. Fresh, and the Braxtons.
	Douglas gained wider attention with **“Don't Leave Me Hangin',”** which appeared on Beat Factory's *GROOVEssentials Volume One*. The song earned her a nomination for **Best R&B/Soul Recording at the 1998 Juno Awards**. That year, she released her debut album, *Don't Leave Me Hangin'*, through Parlophone.
	Douglas later appeared on Canadian house producers Deko-Ze and Gavo's 2009 single “Explosive!”. Camille Douglas passed in 2025.
