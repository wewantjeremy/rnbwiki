	Brooklyn-born Trinidadian/Jamaican singer and songwriter, Alex Mali is a multi-talented independent artist who puts a breathtaking spin on modern R&B. She writes with an undeniable demonstration of strength in melodic and lyrical ability. The nostalgic relatable feelings of R&B music in the 90s and 2000s reserved only for the likes of our faves like Beyonce, Rihanna, Kelis and more, seemingly resurfaces with Alex’s infectious sound. 
	Out the gate, she scored spots on popular playlists such as Alternative R&B, Breaking R&B, New Music Friday, Fresh Finds and more. In 2019, she released her debut EP Sweet & Sour to an astounding 15 millions streams to date and activating a loyal and growing fanbase including celebrities like Justine Skye and Kylie Jenner who have posted her songs numerous times on social media. She capped off the year setting a tone in the live space opening for artist like Tayla Parx, James Vickery, and Kiana Lede. 
	She has performed online for LiveXLive, Co-Triller, On The Radar and LiveNation Urban as well as hosted her own curated #stayathome live performance on Youtube. Alex’s sophomore EP Phenom shows that she has no intention of slowing down. On Phenom you can hear her growth as a songwriter and vocalist. With songs like the focus track Control and the pop infused song Good Good, Alex has created her own style of Caribbean infused R&B that is undeniably genius. 
In 2021, she released the r&b album Iconic. In 2023, she followed it up with the dance/dancehall/afrobeats inspired album Trust the Process. In 2024, she returned to r&b. directing her visual album Sweet & Spicy, shot on iPhone.
