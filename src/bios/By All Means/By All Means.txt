	By All Means were a Los Angeles trio: James “Jimmy” Varner, Lynn Roderick, and Billy Sheppard. Varner sang and played brass; Roderick sang; Sheppard sang/played guitar and had previously been in the Skool Boyz. Varner and Roderick met while touring with Bill Withers around 1985, and producer Stan Sheppard brought Billy into the picture.
	They released three albums: 1988's self-titled album, 1989's Beyond a Dream, and 1992's It's Real. The first two were released through Island with the final album being distributed by Motown. “I Surrender to Your Love” reached #26 on Billboard's Hot Black Singles chart, and their debut reached #40 on the R&B albums chart. “I'm the One Who Loves You” and “Do You Remember” also reportedly reached #17 R&B, while the 1992 Motown single “The Feeling I Get” reached #48.
	The debut featured some heavy hitters of the era: Paul Jackson Jr. on guitar, Paulinho da Costa on percussion, Gerald Albright and Brandon Fields on sax, and Alfie Silas and Lynne Fiddmont among the backing vocalists.
	By All Means also did songwriting and produced material for Gerald Alston's solo records, which helps explain how they wound up at Motown for It's Real.
	Jimmy Varner and Lynn Roderick are Elle Varner's parents. Both later contributed to her 2012 debut Perfectly Imperfect.
