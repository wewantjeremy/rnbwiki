	1 of the Girls was a female R&B group from Cleveland, Ohio from the early 90’s who has discovered by the late Gerald Levert. The group consisted of members Nina Creque (daughter of jazz pianist, Neal Creque), LaShawn Sykes, Marvelous Miles, and Rae-Deon Kirkland. The group's self-titled album was released in 1993 on EastWest Records America and didn’t chart at all on the Billboard 200. The album featured the single "Do da What", which only achieved moderate success. “Do Da What” peaked at #74 on the Billboard Hot 100, #31 on Billboard’s Rhythmic Top 40 chart and #38 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 13 weeks. Another single, "Handle with Care", which did not chart, was also released from that album. 
	After 1994, it seemed as if the group faded into obscurity. Rae-Deon Kirkland is a gospel singer. Currently, she serves as the Chief of Staff at NASA Glen Research Center in Cleveland and is an active member of the Voices of Koinonia. Nina Creque died in February 2019 from an "undisclosed illness".
