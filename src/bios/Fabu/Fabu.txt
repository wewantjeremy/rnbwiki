	Fabu were an R&B duo consisting of singers Christia Hayes and Janine Williams from Detroit, Michigan. Not much information is given about them, but they were signed to Big Beat Records.
	In 1995, the duo released their first and only single, “Just Roll” which peaked at #88 on the Billboard Hot 100  and #20 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 20 weeks.
	After that, it seems as if Fabu has faded into obscurity. Nothing much has been heard from the girls since.
