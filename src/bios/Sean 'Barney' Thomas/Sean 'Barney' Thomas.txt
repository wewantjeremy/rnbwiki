	Sean “Barney” Thomas, better known as Barney Rubble, was one of the key musicians behind 
Death Row Records' signature G-funk sound in the 1990s. As a keyboardist, producer, and songwriter, he helped create the smooth, funk-driven grooves that powered countless West Coast 
hip-hop classics. He worked on Doggystyle (Snoop Dogg), Regulate... Gfunk Era (Warren G), Converstaion (Twinz) and All Eyez on Me (2pac). He also released music as Soul Affect, as well as with the trio Rhythym & Knowledge. His solo album for Death Row has gone unreleased. It seems that he sporadically releases music as Sean Christopher now.
