	U-Mynd were a 90's R&B recording group from Chicago. Originally a quintet comprised of William Larkin, Jermaine Scott, Marcus Miller (not to be confused with the jazz bassist and former The Jamaica Boys member), Anthony Frederick, and Gavin T. Henderson, U-Mynd signed with 2 Live Crew leader, Luther Campbell's Luke Records. Their debut album, Prove My Love, was released on October 18, 1993. The two singles from that were "Stop, Look, & Listen (To Your Heart)" and the title track, both made their entrances onto Billboard's R&B Singles chart at #106 and #112 respectively. The group also featured on the various artists Christmas album, Christmas at Luke's House, singing and recording their covers of "Silent Night", "This Christmas", and "Will You Be There For Christmas", as well as performing a cover of "We Bring You Joy" with Campbell (as Luke), H-Town, and Elder Chris Brinson and The Gospel Music Ministry Choir. It's uncertain which two members would leave after, but the five-piece was scaled down to a three-piece and the remaining members had moved on to Lil' Joe Records by the second album, Funky, Sexual, Freaky and on the Real, was released on February 25, 1997. One single released from that, "Your Only Lover" reached #105 on Billboard's R&B Singles chart.
