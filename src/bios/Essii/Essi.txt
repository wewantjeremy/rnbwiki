Essii, born March 28, 2002, is an English singer, vocalist, entertainer, artist, performer, and musician who is primarily known for her collaboration with the artist Coi Leray on the song "Brown Eyes." She has also released several other tracks like "I Wanna" and "My Side."
She started her essiiofficial YouTube channel in November 2016. The first video on her channel is titled Essii - Spicy (Official Video). She enjoys spending time on the beach. In June 2024, her song "Let U Go" was played on the popular radio station, BBC Radio 1. In March of 2025, she performed live at South by Southwest (SXSW) in Austin, Texas. She has Zambian heritage.
