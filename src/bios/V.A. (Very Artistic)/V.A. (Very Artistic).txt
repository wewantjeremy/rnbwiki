	V.A., also known as Very Artistic were an R&B trio signed to DreamWorks Records circa 2000-2001. They are from Richmond, Virginia, hence VA. Not much is known about them. Advanced copies of their first album, One By One, were distributed, but the album did not see a commercial release. Troy Taylor, along with his production partner Charles Farrar (known together as The Characters), and prolific production duo Tim & Bob, were heavily involved in the album's creation. It seems DreamWorks folding into Universal Music hurt the groups trajectory.
