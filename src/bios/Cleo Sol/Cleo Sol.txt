	Cleo Sol, born Cleopatra Zvezdana Nikolic on March 24, 1990, in Ladbroke Grove, West London, is an acclaimed English R&B and soul singer-songwriter. She is best known for her introspective solo albums and her role as the lead vocalist for the mysterious British music collective Sault. Born to musical parents, Cleo grew up immersed in a diverse soundscape of reggae, soul, jazz, and Motown. Her Jamaican father is a bassist and pianist, while her Serbian-Spanish mother is a singer who plays guitar and flute. As a teenager, she began posting her self-taught songwriting efforts on MySpace, which eventually led to early pop-soul features in the late 2000s. Following a brief hiatus, Cleo Sol returned to the music scene in 2018 with a more artful, soul-rooted sound. She achieved widespread critical acclaim for her solo projects, working closely with her husband, the highly influential producer Inflo. She has released five celebrated solo studio albums: Winter Songs (2018), Rose in the Dark (2020), Mother (2021), Heaven (2023), and Gold (2023). Beyond her solo work, Cleo is the primary vocalist for Sault, an eclectic collective that blends R&B, house, and gospel. With Sault, she has contributed to numerous albums, performed a historic 5-hour headline set at London's All Points East in 2025, and provided vocals on the 2026 surprise album Chapter 1.
