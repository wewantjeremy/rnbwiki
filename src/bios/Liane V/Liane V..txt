	Liane V. is a social star, singer and model who has amassed over 4.4 million followers on Instagram. She runs a self-titled YouTube channel where she has posted her music as well as videos recapping different life events. She has hosted red carpet events for The Voice, The X Factor, and American Idol.
	She recorded R&B-leaning pop on and off through the 2010s, including an album produced by the MIDI Mafia.
	She was an athlete in school, competing in gymnastics, cheerleading, and basketball. Music was the one true dream of hers, though, and soon after promoting herself online through YouTube and social media, she was signed by the production company Brand X.
	She has over 11 million fans on her LianeV TikTok account, where she has posted a range of comedic dance and lip sync videos as well as short sketches. She also had over 4 million followers on Vine before the app was shut down.
	She was born in San Jose, California raised in Modesto, California, and later moved to Los Angeles. Her parents ran a DJ company on the side. She began dating Don Benjamin in 2015, and they became engaged in August 2019; the couple wed on August 24, 2021. In January 2023, the couple welcomed a daughter named Zaia. In January 2025, they announced they were expecting their second child together. In June 2025, they welcomed their son Zaiden Don Benjamin. She and Don have run the YouTube channel Forever Us together. She also previously dated actor and Vine star King Bach. She's appeared in music videos for artists like Tyga and Far East Movement.
