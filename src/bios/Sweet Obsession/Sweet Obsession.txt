	Sweet Obsession was a 1980s and 1990s R&B trio comprised of Detroit-born sisters Keena, Kimmala, and Michelle Green. Known for their smooth vocal harmonies, the group released a well-received self-titled debut in 1988 and a highly regarded sophomore project, Sweet Obsession Too (1991). Sweet Obsession (1988) peaked at #45 on the Billboard R&B Albums chart. It featured two massive top-10 R&B hits: "Gonna Get Over You" (which peaked at #8) and "Being in Love Ain't Easy" (which peaked at #10). Sweet Obsession Too (1991), their sophomore project, is remembered for tracks like "Elevator" and "I'm a Good Woman Looking for a Good Man". While their uptempo tracks defined their initial U.S. radio success, their mid-tempo and soul-heavy cuts, such as "Give Me All My Love Back" and "I Want to Know," gained a cult-classic reputation overseas. Across modern soul and R&B communities, critics and listeners frequently praise these mid-tempo deep cuts as "London stepper classics" that brilliantly bridged the late-80s hip-hop generation and 90s contemporary R&B sounds.
