	Menargi is an R&B quartet from New York City featuring members Anthony Rodriguez, Tito, A.D., and Kevin. History: The foursome signed with Columbia and Soul Conventions Records, releasing their lone album, All Day Every Day, in 1994. They are best known for their single "Now I Realize", which charted on Billboard's R&B Singles. The late Fat Boys member Prince Markie Dee served as one of the executive producers for the album.
