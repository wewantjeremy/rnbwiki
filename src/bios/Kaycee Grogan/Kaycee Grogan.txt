	Kaycee Grogan (born in 1980 in Marietta, Georgia) is an R&B music singer from the ’90s. As a teenager, she was signed to Columbia Records. In 1996, she released her debut single, “It’s Alright” which peaked at #58 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 14 weeks. In 1997, Kaycee released her debut studio album, “What Girls Are Made Of” in Japan, but for unknown reasons, it was never officially released in the United States (although promotional copies appear to exist). The follow-up single released from the album, “So In Love” didn’t appear to make any impact on the charts.
	In later years, Kaycee attended Kennesaw State University where she received a degree in Theatre & Performance Studies with a minor in Dance. She also appeared in various plays in the metro-Atlanta such as “Ain’t Misbehavin'”, “Hairspray,” “Gifts of the Magi”, “Dreamgirls” and “The Color Purple”.
	She worked as a vocal producer & songwriter for the OWN Network television series, “Greenleaf” during the show’s second and third seasons which earned her an NAACP Image Award, Stellar Gospel Music Award, a Grammy Award nomination for her song, “The Judgement” and a Grammy Award for singer Le’Andria Johnson’s song, “Better Days”.
	These days (according to research), Kaycee is married with three children. She works as a theatre teacher for the Marietta City Schools district where she received the title for “Teacher of the Year” in 2013. She is also signed to the Dominion Entertainment Group.
