	J'son (born Jason Tyrel Thomas) is an American contemporary R&B singer who achieved commercial success in the mid-to-late 1990s while signed to Hollywood Records. He is most famously recognized for his 1996 hit single "I'll Never Stop Loving You," which peaked at #62 on the Billboard Hot 100 and was famously covered by Britney Spears as a B-side track to her hit "(You Drive Me) Crazy".
	Growing up in South Central Los Angeles, J'son began singing on street corners for tips. He caught his big break at age 13 when he was discovered by a manager, leading to a record deal with Hollywood Records. He went on to release his self-titled debut album, J'son, on February 27, 1996.
	Following a shelved sophomore solo project due to lackluster sales of his 1998 single "I Should Cheat on You", J'son transitioned into the prominent R&B group 3rd Storee. He joined the established R&B boy band (later known as Chapter 4) and lent his vocals to their 2002 album.
