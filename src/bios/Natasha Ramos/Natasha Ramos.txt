	Natasha started singing at a very early age. She caught the attention of Linda Casey through a talent search in Connecticut that landed her a deal alongside Letoya Luckett and Latavia Roberson (formerly of Destiny's Child) in their new female group Anjell. Natasha sought new opportunities after being in the group about a year. The rumors of her not getting along with other band members are false and should be totally disregarded. She started working with Andre "mrDeyo" Deyo who wrote "Jenny From the Block" and had Natasha sing the demo. JLo loved what she heard and had Natasha record background vocals for the "Thie is me…Then" album. She also kept most of the vocals on JFTB. This helped her land a deal with Arista Records which later folded due to label issues. Through that deal she landed the opportunity to work with Pharell Williams of The Neptunes. He fell in love with her voice and asked her to be a part of an all latina girl group. She accepted and worked alongside Vanessa Marquez. Once a third member was added, the group folded and parted ways. Natasha then signed a solo deal with Star Trak Entertainment and has been singing her story ever since.
	She is commonly confused with ex-group mate Vanessa Marquez and Tasha, the "So Sick" singer signed to Jive Records. Natasha has had a slew of underground hits such as "Midnight Hour", "Invisible", and "Strawberries". Other underground songs include "Who Needs You", "Ride With You", "Got Me On Lock", and "This Love" and various backgrounds vocals with artists such Jennifer Lopez and others. Formerly a member of the R&B group Anjel.
