	JoJo Robinson (born December 30, 1995) is a South African reality TV star, wildlife photographer, and artist. She gained widespread fame as a breakout cast member on the hit reality series The Real Housewives of Durban and also starred in The Real Housewives Ultimate Girls Trip: Africa. Personal Life & CareerBorn and raised in South Africa, JoJo is married to prominent businessman and former professional kickboxer Calven Robinson. The couple is known for their luxurious lifestyle and resides in Durban, South Africa. She is a mother to a son from a previous relationship. Beyond television, JoJo is an accomplished artist and wildlife photographer who frequently shares her extensive tattoo collection and creative projects with her large social media following. She joined the second season of The Real Housewives of Durban and quickly became a fan favorite for her humor, direct personality, and lavish homes.
