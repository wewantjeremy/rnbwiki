	Damozel is the name of a R&B quartet from Los Angeles, California comprising Lisa, Tasha, Tamisha and Brandy. The group's name was taken from the French name 'Damoiselle' which means 'Young lady'. In their early teens they scored a hit with their debut single 'You Don't Know Me Like That' in 2000 taken from their shelved debut album of the same name for MCA. Advance copies of the CD are now an R&B collector's item. Their 2001 single 'Everyday's A Party' was a smash club anthem produced by then-newcomer Eddie Berkeley which was also taken from the "Two Can Play That Game" soundtrack. The group continued recording but still have not released an album.
