	Twelve A.M. were a five-man Southern California R&B vocal group: Marcus “Chill” Phillips, Tracy “Tramp” Johnson, Earnest “E.J.” Riggs, Victor “VO VO” Johnson, and Gerald “Baby Boy” Williams. They were a quintet associated with At-Last V.I.P. Entertainment, a Southern California label connected with iconic Long Beach store/studio V.I.P. Records. The Time Has Come, released 1995, is their only album.
