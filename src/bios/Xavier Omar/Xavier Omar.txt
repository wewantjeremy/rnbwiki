	Xavier Omär is an R&B artist, singer and songwriter out of San Antonio, Texas. From his early work with producer Sango, to his hit song ‘Blind Man’, all the way to an appearance on Barack Obama’s Favorite Music of 2022 playlist with his song ‘Feelings 4 You’, Xavier Omär has captivated his supporters with vulnerable music that tugs at the heart. He describes his music and purpose in one sentence; “I make music about love & relationships with God’s perspective.” With a mix of influences such as James Blake, Pharrell, and The 1975, his diverse sound and songwriting redefine the limits of modern R&B.
