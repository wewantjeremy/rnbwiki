	Sista was an American R&B girl group active in the early 1990s, best known as the launching pad for the legendary rapper, singer, and producer Missy Elliott. Originally formed in Virginia as Fayze, the group included members Missy Elliott, LaShawn Shellman, Chonita Coleman, and Radiah Scott.They were discovered by DeVante Swing of the R&B group Jodeci and signed to his Swing Mob imprint under Elektra Records. Sista released advance copies of their only studio album, 4 All the Sistas Around da World, in September 1994, which featured co-production by a then-unknown Timbaland. Although the album received positive reviews from music critics, the work was eventually shelved due to their first single "Brand New," having little success on the charts. However, in May 2017, 4 All the Sistas Around da World was officially released on digital and streaming platforms by Rhino Entertainment. Paul Clifford of AllMusic called the effort a "superb and extremely underrated album," noting the song "Feel of Your Lips" as the highlight.
