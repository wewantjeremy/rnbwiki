	Me-2-U was an R&B group from Washington, DC that consisted of members Jerry Lattisaw (the younger brother of R&B singer Stacy Lattisaw), DeVaughn Howard, Eric Sanders, Damon Dunnock and Tony Dumas. In 1993, the group released their one and only self-titled debut album on RCA Records. It’s unknown how or if the album ever charted. The first single from the album “Want U Back” peaked at #112 on Billboard’s Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles chart and #45 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 12 weeks. The second single “All Night” peaked at #57 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 14 weeks. The last single “Raindrops” failed to chart. The group later went their separate ways, but continued to stay active in the music business.
	Jerry Lattisaw and Tony Dumas went on to join a group called Majusty (the name was later changed to Prophet Jones) and released their self-titled album in 2001.
	Devaughn Howard and Eric Sanders formed a group called Diversiti and released their self-titled album in 2002. They released solo albums under the names “Devon Howard” and “Eriq J’Mar” respectively.
