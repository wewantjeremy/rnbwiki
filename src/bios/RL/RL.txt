	RL (born Robert Lavelle Huggar) is an American R&B singer and songwriter best known as the frontman of the multi-platinum 1990s R&B trio Next. Boasting a distinct, passionate lead voice, he helped propel the group to global stardom with massive hits like "Too Close" and "Wifey" before establishing himself as a highly successful solo artist and sought-after songwriter for other major industry names. Born on April 2, 1977, in Minneapolis, Minnesota, RL grew up surrounded by the city's rich musical heritage. In the mid-1990s, he teamed up with brothers Terry "T-Low" Brown and Raphael "Tweet" Brown to form Next. Discovered by producer KayGee of Naughty by Nature, the group signed to Arista Records and launched their debut album Rated Next (1997). RL’s lead vocals on the smash single "Too Close" drove the track to #1 on the Billboard Hot 100, ultimately becoming the biggest song of 1998. The group followed up with their sophomore album Welcome II Nextasy (2000), which birthed another massive #1 R&B anthem, "Wifey". 	As Next’s primary frontman, RL easily transitioned into high-profile solo work and collaborations In 1999, he teamed up with Case, Tyrese, and Ginuwine for the iconic R&B super-collaboration "The Best Man I Can Be" for The Best Man movie soundtrack. He scored another major Billboard smash in 1999, duetting with singer Deborah Cox on the chart-topping R&B ballad "We Can't Be Friends." In 2002, RL released his debut solo album, RL: Ements, via Clive Davis’s J Records. The album featured the successful singles "Good Man" and "Do U Wanna Roll" (featuring Snoop Dogg and Lil' Kim). Beyond his own vocal performances, RL established himself as a prominent songwriter and producer behind the scenes. He has penned hits, written album cuts, and collaborated with an elite roster of artists, including Jaheim ("Just in Case", "Anything"), Usher, Keith Sweat, and Luther Vandross.
