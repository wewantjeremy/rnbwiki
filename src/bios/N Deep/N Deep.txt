	N Deep was a male R&B trio based in Los Angeles. They released a lone self-titled album in 1998 through Back Bone productions. Lost to time, not much is known about them. The group collaborated with Teena Marie, in a duet between Marie and N Deep member Franky. "Can You Call a Rose by Any Other Name?" was recorded again by Teena Marie in 2004 as a duet with Gerald Levert, for her album La Doña.
