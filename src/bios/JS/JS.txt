	JS (an acronym for the Johnson Sisters) was an American female R&B duo consisting of sisters Kim Johnson and Kandy Johnson-Isley. They are best known for their 2003 debut studio album, Ice Cream, which was spearheaded by the hit, sensual title track "Ice Cream". The sisters were discovered and signed to DreamWorks Records by R&B legend R. Kelly and Ronald Isley (Kandy would later marry Ronald Isley). In July 2003, they released their debut and only studio album, Ice Cream. The project was heavily written and produced by R. Kelly. The album spawned two well-known singles: the hit single "Ice Cream" and "Love Angel", which featured R. Kelly. Despite the buzz around their debut, the album experienced subpar commercial performance. The duo ultimately disbanded shortly after.
