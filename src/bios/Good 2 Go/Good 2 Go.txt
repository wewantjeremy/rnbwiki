	Good 2 Go was an all-female R&B/dance group from Los Angeles that consisted of members Melissa Miller, Natalie Fernie, Kathy Webb, Cindy Shows and Missy Newman. In 1992, the group released their self-titled debut album on Giant Records. The album didn’t chart at all on the Billboard 200 or R&B albums chart.
	The group’s biggest hit to date “Never Satisfied” peaked at #64 on the Billboard Hot 100 and #10 on Billboard’s Hot R&B Singles chart, staying on the chart for 19 weeks.
	The follow-up single “He Thinks He’s All That” was released, but didn’t appear to make the charts at all.
