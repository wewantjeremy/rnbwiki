	AC Black was an R&B music group from Macon, Georgia that consisted of lead singer Kelvin Jones, multi-instrumentalist Tracey Orr and keyboardist/programmer Brian Haught. The group formed in 1985 by identical twins Alvin and Calvin Black.
	In 1989, they released their debut and only self-titled album on Taj/Motown Records. The album didn’t appear to make any impact on the album charts at all. The only single released from the album, “Funky Situation” flopped on the charts. By 1992, AC Black disbanded and faded into obscurity. It’s unknown what the members are currently up to these days.
