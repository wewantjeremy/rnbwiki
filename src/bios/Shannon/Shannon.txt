	Shannon Brenda Greene, is an American singer-songwriter born May 2, 1958 in Washington, D.C. Shannon hit gold with the trendsetting, pulsating dance classic "Let the Music Play" and other similar-sounding hits in the mid-'80s that helped lay the foundation for the '90s electronica music genre. In 1998, Shannon teamed up with German producer/artist Sash! and released "Move Mania", which marked the start of her comeback. Following the remixes of "Give Me Tonight" and "Let The Music Play" in 1999 and 2000, the album The Best Is Yet To Come was released in 2000 in the US.
