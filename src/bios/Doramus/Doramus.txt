	Doramus (full name Doramus Roberts) is primarily known in the music industry as a prominent R&B and gospel songwriter and producer rather than a lead vocalist. He has worked behind the scenes with various established acts and producers, including Blac Elvis. Doramus is credited on numerous R&B and gospel records, including working on songs for artists like Jovanie, Kenny Lattimore, and the R&B group Brutha. While primarily a producer, he has been featured as a vocalist on mellow R&B tracks, such as Amber Houston's song "Rather Be Alone". He has also released his own material and R&B singles, such as "Pass It Up" and "Freaky Mood".
