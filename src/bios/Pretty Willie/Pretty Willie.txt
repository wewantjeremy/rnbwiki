	Pretty Willie, whose real name is William Moore Jr., is an American R&B and hip-hop singer, comedian, and radio host from St. Louis, Missouri. He is best known for his early 2000s hits like "4 Walls," "She Got a Man at Home," and "Lay Your Body Down". Today, he goes by Willie "Pdub" Moore Jr. and has transitioned from secular music to become a major figure in Christian and inspirational entertainment. He hosts a nationally syndicated radio show, runs the "Young Fly and Saved" youth movement, and has won awards for his media and advocacy work.
